Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a blocked drain repair cost in Dubai?

▼

Blocked drain repair in Dubai typically costs between AED 150 and AED 600, depending on the severity of the blockage, drain location, and method required. Simple sink or shower drain unblocking starts around AED 150–250. Deeper blockages in main sewer lines or those requiring hydro-jetting can cost AED 400–600 or more. Emergency call-outs outside normal hours may add a surcharge of AED 100–200.

How long does an unclog drain service take in Dubai?

▼

A standard unclog drain service in Dubai usually takes 30 minutes to 2 hours. A minor kitchen or bathroom blockage can often be cleared in under an hour. More complex blockages involving tree root intrusion, collapsed pipes, or deep sewer line obstructions may take 3–5 hours, especially if CCTV inspection is needed to locate the exact problem.

When should I call an emergency plumber near me for a blocked drain in Dubai?

▼

You should call an emergency plumber near you immediately if you notice sewage backing up into sinks, toilets, or showers, foul odors coming from multiple drains simultaneously, or water pooling on floors with no sign of clearing. These are signs of a main line blockage that can cause property damage if left untreated. Most Dubai plumbing companies offer 24/7 emergency response, typically arriving within 30–90 minutes depending on your location.

What methods are used to unblock drains in Dubai residential properties?

▼

Common drain unblocking methods in Dubai include manual rodding for soft blockages, high-pressure hydro-jetting for grease and debris buildup, chemical drain treatments for minor clogs, and CCTV camera inspection to diagnose blockages inside pipework. Hydro-jetting is the most effective long-term solution and is frequently used in Dubai due to grease accumulation from cooking. The method chosen affects both cost and duration of the job.

How can I tell if my drain blockage is a minor clog or a serious plumbing issue?

▼

A minor clog typically affects only one fixture, such as a single sink or shower, drains slowly, and has no foul smell. A serious plumbing issue is indicated when multiple drains back up at the same time, toilets gurgle when sinks are used, or water rises in unexpected areas. If more than one fixture is affected, this usually points to a main sewer line blockage requiring professional blocked drain repair rather than a simple DIY fix.
